This tool has not been tested clinically and is a proof of concept for research and software demonstration only. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat, or rule out any condition, and its outputs may be wrong, including false positives and false negatives. Do not use it to make decisions about your health. For any medical issue, seek a licensed healthcare professional. In an emergency, go to your nearest emergency room or dial 911 immediately.

PillTray Set-Difference

Photograph everything in the bag or on the tray. PillTray matches the photo only against the patient's own expected list, and shows only the difference: missing, short, extra, unexpected, or ambiguous. A pharmacist or clinician confirms the tray. PillTray never says what anyone should take.
